## SECTION 2: WHAT IS COVERED

### 2.1 Inpatient Hospitalization (Minimum 24-Hour Stay)
1. **Room Rent & Boarding**: Covered up to **Deluxe Room / Suite category** — absolutely no sub-limit or capping.
2. **ICU / ICCU Charges**: **100% covered** — No cap, No sub-limit.
3. **Specialist, Surgeon & Anaesthetist Fees**: 100% of actual charges.
4. **Operation Theatre**: 100% of actual OT charges.
5. **Advanced Diagnostics**: MRI, CT, PET-CT, DSA, Genetic testing for cancer management — fully covered.
6. **Medicines, Blood Products, Consumables**: All prescribed, including branded biologics and monoclonal antibody infusions.
7. **Prosthetics & Implants**: Medically necessary prosthetic devices, stents, valves, orthopedic implants — fully covered.

### 2.2 Pre-Hospitalization Expenses
Medical expenses incurred up to **90 days before** hospital admission for the same illness — fully covered.

### 2.3 Post-Hospitalization Expenses
Medical expenses incurred up to **180 days after discharge** for the same illness — fully covered.

### 2.4 OPD (Outpatient) Coverage — Unique to Premium Plan
Outpatient expenses covered up to **₹15,000 per year** (no hospitalization needed), including:
- Doctor consultations (General Physician and Specialists)
- Diagnostic tests (prescribed blood work, scans, X-rays)
- Pharmacy expenses (prescribed medicines)
- Physiotherapy sessions (up to 20 per year)
- Dental consultation and basic treatment (up to ₹3,000 within OPD limit)

### 2.5 Day Care Procedures (600+ Listed Procedures)
All procedures covered including all items in Gold plan, plus:
- Robotic surgery (Laparoscopic robotic-assisted procedures)
- Stereotactic Radiosurgery (Gamma Knife, CyberKnife)
- Targeted Biologic Therapy infusions
- Bone marrow biopsy and aspiration
- Plasmapheresis / Therapeutic plasma exchange
- Intrathecal chemotherapy administration
- ERCP (Endoscopic Retrograde Cholangiopancreatography)

### 2.6 Maternity Benefit (No Waiting Period for Renewals Beyond 2 Years; Fresh Policies: 12-Month Wait)
1. **Normal Delivery**: Covered up to **₹1,50,000** per delivery.
2. **Caesarean Section (C-Section)**: Covered up to **₹2,50,000** per delivery.
3. **Complications of Pregnancy**: Pre-eclampsia, Eclampsia, Placenta Praevia, Postpartum haemorrhage — fully covered within maternity limit.
4. **Pre-Natal Care**: Consultations, ultrasounds (anomaly scan, Doppler, growth scans), antenatal blood work — covered.
5. **Post-Natal Care**: 30 days post-delivery follow-up covered.
6. Applicable for up to **3 deliveries** across policy lifetime.

### 2.7 Newborn Baby Cover
Newborn covered from **Day 1** for up to **6 months** including:
- Neonatal ICU (NICU) charges
- Phototherapy, surfactant therapy
- Congenital disease treatment (non-exclusionary for first 6 months)
- Neonatal vaccinations as per IAP schedule

### 2.8 Mental Health & Psychiatric Coverage
As mandated by the Mental Healthcare Act 2017 and IRDAI circular, the plan provides:
- **Inpatient psychiatric hospitalization**: Covered in full (Depression requiring hospitalization, Bipolar disorder acute episodes, Schizophrenic breaks, Anxiety disorder with hospitalization, OCD with severe disability, Eating disorders with medical complications).
- **Telepsychiatry sessions**: 10 sessions per year under OPD limit.
- **De-addiction treatment** for recognized medical conditions: Covered up to ₹50,000 per year (residential programs at NABH-accredited centers).

### 2.9 Critical Illness Lump Sum Payout (In Addition to Hospitalization Cover)
On first confirmed diagnosis of any of the following critical illnesses, a **lump sum of ₹5,00,000** is paid immediately regardless of actual treatment cost (subject to 90-day survival post diagnosis):
1. Cancer (any malignancy, all stages)
2. Heart Attack (First Myocardial Infarction)
3. Coronary Artery Bypass Graft (CABG) surgery
4. Stroke resulting in permanent neurological deficit
5. Major organ transplant (Kidney, Heart, Liver, Lung, Bone Marrow)
6. Kidney Failure requiring permanent dialysis
7. Multiple Sclerosis (confirmed with MRI evidence)
8. Motor Neuron Disease (ALS/Lou Gehrig's)
9. Aorta Graft Surgery
10. Aplastic Anaemia (requiring bone marrow transplant)

### 2.10 International Emergency Cover
Emergency hospitalization outside India covered up to **₹10,00,000** per occurrence (subject to emergency admission within 30 days of departure from India).

### 2.11 Global Medical Second Opinion
One complimentary medical second opinion per year from an internationally accredited specialist at no additional cost — available via telemedicine.

### 2.12 AYUSH Treatment
Full coverage for inpatient AYUSH treatments at NABH/NABL-accredited hospitals — no sub-limit.

### 2.13 Ambulance
- Emergency Road Ambulance: Covered up to **₹10,000 per hospitalization**.
- Air Ambulance: Covered up to **₹3,00,000 per policy year** (for life-threatening emergencies).

### 2.14 Unlimited Sum Insured Restoration
Sum Insured is restored **unlimited number of times** in the policy year — including for the **same illness**. This ensures continuous protection even for cancer, organ failure, or prolonged ICU stays.

### 2.15 Annual Wellness Benefits
- Annual comprehensive health check-up covered up to **₹10,000** per insured.
- Gym / Fitness membership reimbursement: **₹5,000 per year** (at empanelled wellness centres).
- Nutrition counselling sessions: 4 sessions per year.

## SECTION 3: WHAT IS NOT COVERED (EXCLUSIONS)

### 3.1 Waiting Periods
- **PED Waiting Period**: **12 months** (shortest in industry — reduced from standard 24 months).
- **Specific Ailment Waiting Period**: **12 months** for Cataract, Hernia, Hydrocele, Joint Replacement (elective), Gallstones, Varicose veins, Piles.
- **Initial Waiting Period**: **15 days** (reduced from standard 30 days).
- **Maternity Waiting Period**: **12 months** for fresh policies (nil for renewals beyond 2 years).

### 3.2 Permanent Exclusions
1. Cosmetic / aesthetic surgery (unless post-accident reconstructive).
2. Experimental or unapproved treatments (outside clinical trials for life-threatening diseases).
3. War, nuclear, radiological, or chemical warfare.
4. Deliberate self-harm or suicide attempts.
5. Extreme adventure sports not in IRDAI-approved list.
6. Infertility, IVF, ICSI, IUI — assisted reproduction.
7. Dental implants and orthodontic braces (OPD dental basic covered within OPD limit).
8. Refractive (vision correction) LASIK for errors below 7.5 dioptres.
