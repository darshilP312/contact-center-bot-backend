## POLICY OVERVIEW & SPECIALTY

**Motor Third Party (TP) Liability Policy** is the legally mandatory minimum motor insurance required for every vehicle registered in India. It protects the vehicle owner against **financial liabilities arising from injury, death, or property damage caused to a third party** (other person) by the insured vehicle. This policy does **NOT** cover the insured vehicle itself.

**Key Specialty / USPs:**
- **Legally mandatory** — driving without TP insurance is a punishable offence (fine up to ₹2,000 and/or imprisonment up to 3 months).
- **Unlimited personal liability** for third-party bodily injury / death (as determined by courts).
- **Compulsory Personal Accident cover** of **₹15 Lakhs** for the registered owner-driver.
- Covers all passengers' third-party injuries.
- Extremely affordable premium regulated by IRDAI — no variation between insurers.

## SECTION 1: WHAT IS COVERED

### 1.1 Third-Party Bodily Injury & Death (Unlimited Liability)
The Company indemnifies the Insured for **all sums legally payable** to a third party (pedestrian, cyclist, vehicle occupant of another vehicle, or any other person not in the insured vehicle) as compensation for:
1. **Death** caused by or arising out of the use of the insured vehicle.
2. **Bodily Injury** — including fractures, permanent disability, temporary disability, or grievous hurt.
3. Compensation is determined by:
   - **Motor Accident Claims Tribunal (MACT)** or a Court of Law.
   - **Structured Formula (Workmen's Compensation Act)** for occupational class claimants.
   - **MACT interim compensation** payable within 30 days of application even before final award.
4. **No upper cap** on the compensation amount — liability is unlimited per Indian law.

### 1.2 Third-Party Property Damage
Damage caused to a third party's property (another car, shop, boundary wall, street lamp, public infrastructure) is covered up to:
- **₹7,50,000 (₹7.5 Lakhs)** per accident.

### 1.3 Compulsory Personal Accident (PA) Cover for Owner-Driver
**Mandatory by law** — Provides a capital sum insured of **₹15,00,000 (₹15 Lakhs)** for:

| Nature of Loss | % of Capital Sum Insured Payable |
|---|---|
| Death | 100% — ₹15,00,000 |
| Loss of both hands or sight in both eyes | 100% — ₹15,00,000 |
| Loss of one hand and one eye | 100% — ₹15,00,000 |
| Loss of one hand or one eye (single limb / eye) | 50% — ₹7,50,000 |
| Permanent Total Disablement (from injury) | 100% — ₹15,00,000 |

**Eligibility**: The registered owner of the vehicle must hold a **valid, effective driving licence** at the time of the accident.

### 1.4 Legal Defence Costs
Reasonable costs incurred by the Insured in defending claims made before the MACT or Civil Court arising from a third-party accident are covered within the total liability indemnified.

### 1.5 Coverage During:
- Driving on public roads, highways, flyovers, parking lots, and private roads open to public.
- Vehicle stationary but involved in an accident.
- While the vehicle is being towed (first vehicle only).

## SECTION 2: WHAT IS NOT COVERED (EXCLUSIONS)

### 2.1 Damage to Own Vehicle — NOT COVERED
The Motor Third Party policy does **NOT cover** any loss or damage to the **insured vehicle itself**. For coverage of own vehicle, a **Comprehensive Motor Policy** is required.

### 2.2 Specific Exclusions
The Company shall not be liable under any circumstance for:
1. **Driving under influence**: Accident caused while the driver was under the influence of alcohol, drugs, or any intoxicant.
2. **Unlicensed driver**: Accident caused while the vehicle was being driven by a person not holding a valid, effective driving licence.
3. **Contractual liability**: Any liability assumed by the Insured under a contract that exceeds statutory liability.
4. **Intentional act**: Deliberate use of vehicle to cause harm.
5. **Use outside permitted scope**: Use of vehicle for racing, speed testing, or purposes not described in the policy schedule.
6. **Nuclear / War Risks**: Damage or liability arising from nuclear weapons, biological warfare, invasion, or civil war.
7. **Damage to goods being carried**: Property being transported in the vehicle is excluded.
8. **Non-motorized**: Vehicles without an engine (cycle, push cart) are excluded.
9. **Outside India**: Liability arising outside the territorial limits of India (Nepal and Bhutan included if specifically endorsed).

### 2.3 Passenger Cover
PA cover for passengers **is not included** in this base policy but can be purchased as an **Add-On Rider** (Passenger PA Add-On) at an additional premium.

## SECTION 3: CLAIMS PROCEDURE

### 3.1 Immediate Steps After an Accident
1. **Do not flee** the scene — Indian law requires stopping and reporting the accident.
2. File an **FIR (First Information Report)** at the nearest Police Station for any accident involving injury or death.
3. Inform SecureShield within **24 hours** of the accident via the 24x7 claims helpline: **1800-XXX-XXXX**.
4. Cooperate with the Police investigation and MACT proceedings.
5. **Do not admit liability** or make any payment to the third party directly without authorization from the Company.

### 3.2 Claims Management
- The Company appoints a **Legal Representative / Solicitor** to defend all MACT/court proceedings at no cost to the Insured.
- MACT Interim Relief: Insurer deposits interim compensation with the Tribunal directly on behalf of the Insured.
- Final MACT Award / Court Decree: Insurer directly pays the claimant / court as directed.

### 3.3 Documents Required
- Copy of FIR (for injury/death accidents)
- Driving licence of the driver at time of accident
- Vehicle Registration Certificate (RC)
- Original Motor TP Insurance Policy
- Panchnama / Spot Mahazar (issued by Police)
- Medical reports for injured party / Death Certificate
- MACT petition / summons served
