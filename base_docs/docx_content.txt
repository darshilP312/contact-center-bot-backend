Why this architecture works
The architecture intentionally separates conversation, reasoning, and business execution:
Speech services handle audio only.
The Conversation Orchestrator owns state, memory, and workflow progression.
The LLM is used for understanding, planning within constraints, and natural-language generationΓÇönot for executing business logic.
Tools perform deterministic actions against enterprise systems.
RAG answers knowledge questions, while tools execute transactions.
Policies enforce business and compliance rules before actions are taken.
This mirrors the design principles used in production AI contact centers while remaining compact enough for a demo. More importantly, every major AI capabilityΓÇöstreaming conversation, memory, orchestration, tool use, retrieval, and workflow automationΓÇöis visible to the audience, making it a strong showcase of enterprise AI rather than just another voice chatbot.
