# TELCONET ENTERPRISE SERVICES — CUSTOMER SERVICE, BILLING & OUTAGE CREDIT POLICY
**Document Ref: TN-OPS-POL-2024/REV.3**

---

### SECTION 1: BROADBAND SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT (SLA) & OUTAGE CREDITS

#### Clause 1.1: Guaranteed Uptime & Pro-Rata Credit
TelcoNet guarantees a minimum network uptime of 99.5% for all residential and commercial fibre broadband lines.
1. If a verified continuous area outage or optical line fault exceeds **24 hours**, the customer is entitled to a **pro-rata daily rental credit** on their next billing cycle.
2. For severe outages lasting more than **72 consecutive hours**, customers may request a full pro-rata monthly credit or waiver of line rental for that billing month.

#### Clause 1.2: Standard Troubleshooting Sequence
Before an on-site field technician dispatch:
1. **Primary Step**: Power cycle router / ONT optical terminal (unplug for 30 seconds, plug back in).
2. **Secondary Step**: Automated line test & signal refresh from the Session Gateway (verifies Optical Signal-to-Noise Ratio and PON link status).
3. **Escalation**: If the optical LOS (Loss of Signal) light remains solid red or blinking after remote line refresh, an on-site technician ticket is dispatched within 4 hours.

### SECTION 2: BILLING DISPUTES, INSTANT CREDITS & REFUND MATRIX

#### Clause 2.1: 30-Day Billing Dispute Window
Customers may dispute any unexpected charge, add-on package fee, or data roaming surcharge within **30 calendar days** from the invoice date.

#### Clause 2.2: Instant Manager / Agent Credit Authorization
1. **Instant Goodwill Credit**: Frontline customer service agents and AI automated workflows are authorized to apply an **instant account credit of up to INR 500** for unrequested digital add-on packs or disputed line charges reported within 30 days.
2. **Refunds Exceeding INR 500**: Requires secondary supervisor approval or invoice audit review, processed to the customer's payment method within **3 to 5 business days**.

---

### SECTION 3: PLAN MIGRATION, CONTRACT CANCELLATION & RETENTION GUIDELINES

#### Clause 3.1: Plan Upgrades and Downgrades
- Customers may upgrade their internet speed tier (e.g. from 100 Mbps to 300 Mbps or 1 Gbps) at any time. The speed change takes effect within **15 minutes** of confirmation.
- Billing is adjusted on a pro-rata basis for the remaining days of the current billing cycle.

#### Clause 3.2: Early Termination & Cancellation Policy
1. Month-to-month plans have **Zero Cancellation Fee**. Service terminates at the end of the current billing cycle.
2. Annual contract plans cancelled before term expiry are subject to an early exit fee equal to 1 month of base plan rental unless cancellation is due to unrectified verified service outages exceeding 7 consecutive days.
3. **Retention Offers**: To prevent churn, retention agents may offer up to a **20% loyalty discount** for 6 months or a complimentary speed upgrade for existing subscribers.

---

### SECTION 4: UNRESOLVED COMPLAINTS & SUPERVISOR ESCALATION

#### Clause 4.1: Mandatory Escalation Thresholds
A customer session must be immediately transferred to a Senior Human Support Specialist / Floor Supervisor if:
1. The customer has contacted support 2 or more times regarding the same unresolved complaint.
2. The customer explicitly demands to speak with a human agent, supervisor, or manager.
3. The customer expresses strong dissatisfaction, high frustration, or states intent to switch providers / file regulatory consumer complaints.
