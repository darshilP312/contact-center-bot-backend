**Health Shield Basic** is an affordable entry-level individual and family health indemnity plan designed for first-time insurance buyers, young professionals, and price-conscious families. It covers essential inpatient hospitalization expenses with straightforward terms and a wide cashless hospital network.

**Key Specialty:**
- Low-cost quarterly premium with no annual lump-sum burden
- Covers all major acute and chronic illnesses after applicable waiting periods
- 4,500+ cashless network hospitals across India
- Simple paperless digital claims via app or WhatsApp

## SECTION 2: WHAT IS COVERED

### 2.1 Inpatient Hospitalization (Minimum 24-Hour Stay)
The Company indemnifies expenses incurred for hospitalization of at least 24 consecutive hours for:
1. **Room Rent & Boarding**: Covered up to **₹1,500 per day** (General / Semi-Private ward category).
2. **ICU / ICCU Charges**: Covered up to **₹3,000 per day**.
3. **Surgeon, Anaesthetist & Specialist Fees**: 100% of actual charges within Sum Insured.
4. **Operation Theatre Charges**: Covered in full.
5. **Medicines, Drugs & Consumables**: Prescribed medications during inpatient stay.
6. **Diagnostic Tests**: Blood tests, urine tests, X-rays, ECGs, and pathology tests.

### 2.2 Pre-Hospitalization Expenses
Medical expenses incurred up to **30 days before** hospital admission, for the same illness/injury, are covered.

### 2.3 Post-Hospitalization Expenses
Follow-up consultations, prescribed medicines, and diagnostics incurred up to **60 days after discharge** are covered.

### 2.4 Day Care Procedures (No 24-Hour Stay Required)
Coverage for **200+ listed day care procedures** including:
- **Eyes**: Cataract surgery, Phacoemulsification
- **Oncology**: Chemotherapy infusions, Radiotherapy
- **Urology/Nephrology**: Haemodialysis, Lithotripsy for kidney stones
- **ENT**: Tonsillectomy, Nasal polypectomy
- **Orthopaedics**: Arthroscopic surgeries

### 2.5 Emergency Road Ambulance
Ambulance charges covered up to **₹1,500 per hospitalization event**.

### 2.6 Covered Diseases & Conditions
All acute and chronic illnesses requiring hospitalization are covered, including:
- **Cardiovascular**: Heart attack (Myocardial Infarction), Angina, Coronary Artery Disease, Heart valve disorders
- **Respiratory**: Pneumonia, Tuberculosis (TB), Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD), Asthma (hospitalization requiring)
- **Neurological**: Stroke, Epilepsy seizures requiring admission, Meningitis, Encephalitis
- **Cancer / Oncology**: All types of malignant cancers requiring inpatient treatment or chemotherapy/radiotherapy
- **Gastrointestinal**: Appendicitis, Cholecystitis, Intestinal obstruction, Liver disorders, Pancreatitis
- **Orthopaedic**: Fractures, Joint infections, Spinal disorders requiring surgery
- **Infectious Diseases**: Dengue, Malaria, Typhoid, Chikungunya, Leptospirosis, COVID-19 complications
- **Renal / Urological**: Kidney failure requiring dialysis, Urinary tract infections requiring hospitalization, Kidney stones
- **Endocrine**: Diabetes complications (Diabetic Ketoacidosis, Hyperosmolar coma), Thyroid storm
- **Gynaecological**: Ovarian cysts, Ectopic pregnancy (emergency), Uterine fibroids requiring surgery
- **Paediatric Emergencies**: Febrile convulsions, Neonatal jaundice, RSV infections, Dehydration

### 2.7 AYUSH Treatment
Inpatient treatment at Government-recognized AYUSH hospitals is covered within the Sum Insured.

## SECTION 3: WHAT IS NOT COVERED (EXCLUSIONS)

### 3.1 Room Upgrade Proportionate Deduction
If the Insured opts for a room above the **General/Semi-Private** ward (e.g., Private AC Room, Suite), all associated costs (surgeon fees, nursing, consumables) shall be proportionately reduced.

### 3.2 Co-Payment Clause
A mandatory **20% co-payment** applies on each and every claim (i.e., the Insured bears 20% of every admissible claim amount). This co-pay reduces to 10% after 2 continuous claim-free renewal years.

### 3.3 Permanent Exclusions
The following are never covered under Health Shield Basic:
1. **Pre-existing diseases (PED)** in the first **36 months** of continuous coverage.
2. Cosmetic, aesthetic, or reconstructive surgery (unless due to accidental burns or injuries).
3. Dental treatment, unless requiring hospitalization due to accidental jaw fracture.
4. Spectacles, contact lenses, hearing aids, orthopaedic implants for non-injury.
5. Self-inflicted injuries, suicide attempts, or hazardous activities (skydiving, motor racing, bungee jumping).
6. Substance abuse, alcoholism, and related medical conditions.
7. Infertility, IVF, surrogacy, and all assisted reproduction treatments.
8. Treatment received outside India.
9. Experimental or unproven treatments not recognized by IRDAI or Medical Council of India.
10. OPD (Outpatient) consultations, routine health check-ups, and pharmacy visits without hospitalization.
11. Maternity expenses (no maternity cover under Basic plan).

### 3.4 Specific Ailment Waiting Period (24 Months)
The following treatments have a mandatory **24-month waiting period** before they are claimable:
- Cataract surgery, Hernia repair, Hydrocele, Piles / Fistula / Fissure, Benign Prostatic Hypertrophy (BPH), Gallstones & Kidney stones, Varicose veins, Tonsillectomy, Joint replacement surgery, Hysterectomy (non-malignant).

### 3.5 Initial 30-Day Waiting Period
No illness-related claim (except accidental injury) is payable within the first **30 days** from policy start date.

## SECTION 4: CLAIMS PROCEDURE

### 4.1 Cashless at Network Hospitals
- **Planned Hospitalization**: Submit Pre-Authorization request at least **48 hours prior** to admission at the hospital's insurance help desk.
- **Emergency Admission**: Inform Insurer / TPA within **12 hours** of admission.
- **Network**: 4,500+ cashless empanelled hospitals across India.

### 4.2 Reimbursement at Non-Network Hospitals
1. Pay hospital bills in full and collect: Discharge Summary, itemized bills, prescriptions, and diagnostic reports.
2. Submit documents within **30 days of discharge**.
3. Reimbursement processed via direct bank transfer within **7–10 working days**.
