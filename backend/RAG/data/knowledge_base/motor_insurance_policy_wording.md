# SECURESHIELD GENERAL INSURANCE COMPANY LIMITED
## PRIVATE CAR PACKAGE POLICY — POLICY WORDING & TERMS OF COVERAGE
**Policy Form IRDAI/NL-GI/MTR-P/P-C/V.2/2024-25**

---

### SECTION I: LOSS OR DAMAGE TO THE VEHICLE INSURED (OWN DAMAGE)

#### Clause 1.1: Scope of Cover
The Company will indemnify the Insured against loss or damage to the vehicle insured and/or its accessories whilst thereon by:
1. Accidental external means or collision.
2. Fire, explosion, self-ignition, lightning, or frost.
3. Burglary, housebreaking, or theft.
4. Riot, strike, malicious act, or terrorist activity.
5. Earthquake (fire and shock damage), flood, typhoon, hurricane, storm, cyclone, hailstorm, tempest, inundation, landslide, or rockslide.
6. Transit by road, rail, inland waterway, lift, elevator, or air.

#### Clause 1.2: Depreciation Scale on Parts for Partial Loss
In the event of a partial loss claim under standard comprehensive coverage (without Zero Depreciation Add-on), the following depreciation schedule applies to replaced parts:
- **Rubber, Nylon, Plastic parts, Tyres, Tubes, Batteries, and Airbags**: 50% fixed depreciation deduction.
- **Glass parts**: Nil (0% depreciation).
- **Fibre glass components**: 30% depreciation deduction.
- **Metallic parts**: Depreciation scaled by vehicle age:
  - Age up to 6 months: Nil
  - Age between 6 months to 1 year: 5%
  - Age between 1 year to 2 years: 10%
  - Age between 2 years to 3 years: 15%
  - Age between 3 years to 4 years: 25%
  - Age between 4 years to 5 years: 35%
  - Age between 5 years to 10 years: 40%
  - Age exceeding 10 years: 50%

#### Clause 1.3: Insured Declared Value (IDV) and Total Loss
The Insured Declared Value (IDV) of the vehicle shall be treated as the 'Sum Insured' throughout the policy period. IDV is fixed based on manufacturer's listed selling price less depreciation based on vehicle age:
- Age up to 6 months: 5% depreciation from listed price.
- Age between 6 months to 1 year: 15% depreciation.
- Age between 1 year to 2 years: 20% depreciation.
- Age between 2 years to 3 years: 30% depreciation.
- Age between 3 years to 4 years: 40% depreciation.
- Age between 4 years to 5 years: 50% depreciation.
- Age exceeding 5 years: Mutually agreed market value between Insurer and Insured.

A vehicle is treated as a Constructive Total Loss (CTL) when the aggregate cost of retrieval and repair exceeds 75% of the IDV.

### SECTION II: LIABILITY TO THIRD PARTIES

#### Clause 2.1: Legal Liability Coverage
The Company will indemnify the Insured in the event of an accident caused by or through the use of the motor vehicle against all sums which the Insured shall become legally liable to pay in respect of:
1. **Death of or bodily injury** to any person including occupants carried in the vehicle: **Unlimited liability** as determined by the Motor Accident Claims Tribunal (MACT) or Court of Law.
2. **Damage to property** belonging to a third party: Covered up to a statutory limit of **INR 7,50,000 (INR 7.5 Lakhs)**.

#### Clause 2.2: Mandatory Personal Accident (PA) Cover for Owner-Driver
Mandatory PA cover provides capital sum insured of **INR 15,00,000 (INR 15 Lakhs)** for 100% death, loss of two limbs or two eyes, or permanent total disablement of the registered owner-driver holding an effective driving licence.

### SECTION III: DEDUCTIBLES AND EXCESS CLAUSES

#### Clause 3.1: Compulsory Deductible
The Insured shall bear an initial compulsory excess of **INR 2,000 per claim** for private cars exceeding 1500 CC engine capacity (or **INR 1,000 per claim** for private cars up to 1500 CC capacity). The insurer pays the balance of the admissible claim amount after deducting the compulsory deductible.

#### Clause 3.2: Voluntary Deductible Discount Scale
Policyholders may opt for a voluntary deductible to receive premium discounts on the Own Damage premium:
- Voluntary Deductible INR 2,500: 20% discount on OD premium (Max INR 750).
- Voluntary Deductible INR 5,000: 25% discount on OD premium (Max INR 1,500).
- Voluntary Deductible INR 7,500: 30% discount on OD premium (Max INR 2,000).
- Voluntary Deductible INR 15,000: 35% discount on OD premium (Max INR 2,500).

---

### SECTION IV: NO CLAIM BONUS (NCB) REGULATIONS

#### Clause 4.1: NCB Entitlement Slabs
No Claim Bonus is awarded to the policyholder for every consecutive claim-free policy year:
- 1 claim-free year: 20% discount on OD premium.
- 2 consecutive claim-free years: 25% discount.
- 3 consecutive claim-free years: 35% discount.
- 4 consecutive claim-free years: 45% discount.
- 5 or more consecutive claim-free years: 50% maximum discount.

#### Clause 4.2: NCB Portability & Renewal Grace Period
1. NCB belongs to the Insured (policyholder), not the vehicle. When selling the vehicle, the NCB certificate can be transferred to a newly purchased vehicle.
2. If a policy lapses without renewal, accumulated NCB is retained for a grace window of **up to 90 days** from the date of expiry. If not renewed within 90 days, accumulated NCB resets to 0%.

### SECTION V: ENDORSEMENTS & SPECIAL ADD-ON WORDINGS

#### Endorsement MTR-ZD-01: Zero Depreciation Cover (Nil-Dep / Bumper-to-Bumper)
In consideration of payment of additional premium, the Company agrees that in the event of an admissible Own Damage claim under Section I, **no deduction for depreciation shall be applied to replacement of plastic, rubber, nylon, fibre glass, glass, and metal parts**.
- The Insured shall receive 100% reimbursement of part replacement costs without depreciation deductions.
- Only the Compulsory Deductible (INR 2,000) remains payable by the Insured.
- Maximum admissible claims under Zero Dep: **2 claims per policy period**.

#### Endorsement MTR-EP-02: Hydrostatic Engine & Gearbox Protection Cover
Covers direct physical damage to internal engine parts, differential, and gearbox assembly caused by:
1. Water ingression (hydrostatic lock during monsoons or waterlogged roads).
2. Leakage of lubricating oil or coolant due to accidental impact.
*(Standard comprehensive policies strictly exclude water ingression hydrostatic lock without this endorsement).*

#### Endorsement MTR-RSA-03: 24x7 Roadside Assistance Cover
Provides round-the-clock nationwide emergency assistance services:
1. Free towing facility up to **50 km** to the nearest authorized workshop.
2. On-site minor mechanical or electrical breakdown repairs.
3. Flat tyre puncture repair and spare wheel change.
4. Battery jump-start assistance.
5. Emergency fuel delivery (up to 5 litres, fuel cost payable at actuals).
6. Locked key retrieval / key lockout assistance.

#### Endorsement MTR-RTI-04: Return to Invoice (RTI) Cover
In the event of a Constructive Total Loss (CTL) or total theft of the vehicle, the Company will pay the original ex-showroom invoice price of the vehicle plus registration charges and road tax paid, rather than the depreciated IDV.

---

### SECTION VI: GENERAL POLICY EXCLUSIONS

The Company shall not be liable to make any payment in respect of:
1. Damage sustained whilst the vehicle is being driven under the influence of intoxicating liquor or drugs.
2. Damage sustained whilst being driven by any person not holding an effective and valid driving license.
3. Any consequential loss, wear and tear, mechanical or electrical breakdown, failure, or breakage.
4. Damage to tyres and tubes unless the vehicle is damaged in the same accident where liability is admitted.
5. Loss or damage outside the geographical limits of India (unless extended by endorsement).
6. Use of the private vehicle for hire, reward, speed testing, racing, or commercial carriage.
7. Engine seizure due to cranking or driving through flood waters without Engine Protection Cover.

### SECTION VII: CLAIMS INTIMATION AND SETTLEMENT PROCEDURE

#### Clause 7.1: Intimation Timelines
- All accidental damage claims must be notified to the Company **within 48 hours** of the occurrence via the 24x7 helpline or mobile app.
- Theft claims and third-party injury accidents require immediate First Information Report (FIR) filing with the Police authorities.

#### Clause 7.2: Surveyor Inspection
- Upon registration of claim intimation, an IRDAI-certified surveyor is appointed within **24 hours**.
- The surveyor inspects the vehicle at the garage, assesses damaged components, takes photographs, and approves the repair estimate.

#### Clause 7.3: Cashless vs. Reimbursement Settlement
1. **Cashless Network Garages**: Direct billing settlement between Insurer and 6,800+ authorized cashless network workshops. Insured pays only compulsory deductible and non-covered consumables (unless Consumables Add-on is active). Repairs commence immediately upon surveyor approval.
2. **Non-Network Reimbursement**: If repaired at a non-network garage, the customer pays the workshop in full, collects original repair invoice, cash receipt, replaced parts bill, and submits documents to the Company. Reimbursement is processed into customer bank account within **7 to 10 working days**.
