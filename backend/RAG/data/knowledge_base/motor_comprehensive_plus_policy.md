## SECTION 1: OWN DAMAGE (OD) COVERAGE

### 1.1 Scope of Cover
The Company indemnifies all loss or damage to the insured vehicle caused by:
1. Accidental external means — collision, rollover, impact.
2. Fire, explosion, self-ignition, lightning.
3. Burglary, housebreaking, or theft.
4. Riot, strike, malicious act, or terrorist activity.
5. Natural calamities: Earthquake (fire and shock), flood, typhoon, hurricane, storm, cyclone, hailstorm, tempest, inundation, landslide, rockslide.
6. Transit: Damage during road, rail, inland waterway, air, or elevator transport.

### 1.2 IDV (Insured Declared Value) — Agreed Reinstatement Option
For vehicles under 3 years old: **Agreed Value (AV) basis available** — IDV agreed at purchase and does not depreciate mid-policy year.

Standard IDV depreciation for others (same scale as Comprehensive):

| Vehicle Age | Depreciation Rate |
|---|---|
| Up to 6 months | 5% |
| 6 months – 1 year | 15% |
| 1 – 2 years | 20% |
| 2 – 3 years | 30% |
| 3 – 4 years | 40% |
| 4 – 5 years | 50% |
| Above 5 years | Mutually agreed |

### 1.3 Zero Depreciation (Nil-Dep) — Included As Standard
No depreciation deducted on any replaced parts — **rubber, nylon, plastic, fibre glass, glass, metal**. The insured receives **100% of actual replacement cost** for all parts.

- **No limit on number of Zero Dep claims** per policy year (unlike standard Comprehensive's 2-claim limit).
- Only the compulsory deductible (₹2,000) remains payable.

### 1.4 Compulsory Deductible
- **₹1,000** for vehicles up to 1500 CC.
- **₹2,000** for vehicles above 1500 CC.

## SECTION 2: ALL BUNDLED ADD-ONS (INCLUDED WITHOUT EXTRA PREMIUM)

### 2.1 Engine & Gearbox Protection
Covers physical damage to:
- Internal engine components
- Gearbox and differential assembly
- Damage caused by water ingression (hydrostatic lock during monsoons, flooded roads)
- Lubricant oil leakage due to accidental impact

### 2.2 Return to Invoice (RTI) Cover
On **Constructive Total Loss (CTL)** (repair cost > 75% IDV) or **outright vehicle theft**:
- Insurer pays the **original on-road price** (ex-showroom price + registration charges + road tax).
- This eliminates the IDV depreciation gap on total loss.
- Valid for vehicles up to **5 years old**.

### 2.3 24x7 Roadside Assistance (Unlimited)
- Free towing up to **100 km** (vs. 50 km in standard).
- On-site minor mechanical/electrical breakdown assistance.
- Flat tyre puncture repair and spare wheel change.
- Battery jump-start.
- Emergency fuel delivery (up to 10 litres; fuel cost at actuals).
- Locked key / lockout assistance.
- Hotel stay benefit: Up to ₹3,000 per day (max 2 days) if vehicle cannot be repaired at site within 24 hours.

### 2.4 Consumables Cover
All consumable items replaced or used during repair are covered:
- Engine oil, gear oil, coolant, brake fluid
- Nuts, bolts, screws, washers
- Air conditioner refrigerant gas
- Grease and lubricants
- Windscreen wiper fluid

### 2.5 Tyre Protect Cover
- Covers tyre and tube damage — burst, cut, or sidewall damage — even without a simultaneous vehicle accident.
- Labour charges for tyre fitting/balancing covered.
- Maximum: **4 tyres per policy year**.

### 2.6 Key and Lock Replacement
Covers replacement costs for:
- Lost or stolen vehicle keys (including remote key fobs, smart keys, laser-cut keys)
- Replacement of door lock cylinders
- Programming of new transponder keys
- Covered up to **₹25,000 per policy year**.

## SECTION 5: WHAT IS NOT COVERED (EXCLUSIONS)

1. **Driving under influence**: Alcohol, narcotics, or psychotropic substances.
2. **Unlicensed driver**: Driver not holding a valid driving licence.
3. **Intentional damage**: Self-caused damage or deliberate destruction of own vehicle.
4. **Mechanical or electrical breakdown**: Failure arising from wear and tear, not an accident.
5. **Consequential loss**: Loss of earnings, loss of use, or any indirect financial loss.
6. **Use outside permitted purpose**: Racing, speed testing, hire/reward carriage, or commercial use of private vehicle.
7. **Nuclear / war risks**: Nuclear weapons, biological warfare, civil war, invasion.
8. **Outside India**: Damage occurring outside India (Nepal/Bhutan can be endorsed separately).
9. **Unlisted accessories**: Aftermarket accessories not declared and listed in the policy schedule.
10. **Depreciation in value**: Any diminution in market value of the vehicle post-repair.

## SECTION 6: CLAIMS PROCEDURE

### 6.1 Intimation
- **Accident damage**: Notify within **24 hours** (vs. 48 hours in standard Comprehensive).
- **Theft**: File FIR immediately + intimate Company within **24 hours**.

### 6.2 Surveyor
- Appointed within **6 hours** of claim registration for urgent cases.
- AI-assisted/app-based self-survey available for claims under ₹75,000.

### 6.3 Cashless Repair (Priority Service)
- 8,500+ authorized network garages including **luxury/premium brand-authorized workshops** (BMW, Audi, Mercedes-Benz, etc.).
- **Priority lane processing** — surveyor approval within 4 hours of vehicle arrival.
- Repair timeline commitment: Standard body repairs completed within **5 working days** or daily hire car benefit of **₹1,500 per day** (max 7 days) provided.

### 6.4 Hire Car Benefit (Unique to Plus Plan)
If the insured vehicle is in a network garage for repair (for more than 24 hours), a **hire car allowance** of **₹1,500 per day (max 7 days per claim)** is provided to cover alternate transport.

### 6.5 Reimbursement
Non-network garage repairs: Submit documents within **30 days**. Settled within **5–7 working days** (faster SLA than standard).
