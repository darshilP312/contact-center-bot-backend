## SECTION 1: OWN DAMAGE (OD) — LOSS OR DAMAGE TO INSURED VEHICLE

### 1.1 Scope of Cover
The Company indemnifies loss or damage to the insured vehicle caused by:
1. **Accidental collision or external impact** — vehicle-to-vehicle, vehicle-to-object, rollover accidents.
2. **Fire, explosion, self-ignition, or lightning.**
3. **Theft** — outright theft of the entire vehicle or theft of key components.
4. **Housebreaking or burglary** of vehicle accessories.
5. **Natural calamities**: Earthquake (fire and shock), flood, typhoon, hurricane, storm, cyclone, hailstorm, tempest, inundation, landslide, rockslide.
6. **Malicious acts**: Riot, strike, vandalism, terrorist act.
7. **Transit**: Damage while vehicle is being transported by road, rail, inland waterway, elevator, or air.

### 1.2 Insured Declared Value (IDV) — The Sum Insured
The **IDV** is the market value of the vehicle and represents the maximum payable on total loss or theft claims. It is fixed at policy inception based on manufacturer's list price less depreciation:

| Vehicle Age | Depreciation Rate on Manufacturer's List Price |
|---|---|
| Up to 6 months | 5% |
| 6 months to 1 year | 15% |
| 1 year to 2 years | 20% |
| 2 years to 3 years | 30% |
| 3 years to 4 years | 40% |
| 4 years to 5 years | 50% |
| Above 5 years | Mutually agreed between insurer and insured |

### 1.3 Total Loss and Constructive Total Loss (CTL)
A vehicle is declared **Total Loss** or **Constructive Total Loss (CTL)** when:
- The aggregate cost of retrieval + repair > **75% of the IDV**.
- In such cases, the full IDV (less any salvage) is payable to the insured.

### 1.4 Depreciation on Parts (Without Zero Dep Add-On)
For partial repair claims, the following depreciation rates apply to replaced parts:

| Part Type | Depreciation Deduction |
|---|---|
| Rubber, Nylon, Plastic, Tyres, Tubes, Batteries, Airbags | 50% (fixed) |
| Glass parts | Nil (0%) |
| Fibre glass components | 30% |
| Metal parts — vehicle < 6 months old | Nil |
| Metal parts — 6 months to 1 year | 5% |
| Metal parts — 1 to 2 years | 10% |
| Metal parts — 2 to 3 years | 15% |
| Metal parts — 3 to 4 years | 25% |
| Metal parts — 4 to 5 years | 35% |
| Metal parts — 5 to 10 years | 40% |
| Metal parts — above 10 years | 50% |

### 1.5 Compulsory Deductible
The Insured bears an initial compulsory excess per OD claim:
- **₹1,000** for private cars up to 1500 CC engine capacity.
- **₹2,000** for private cars above 1500 CC.

## SECTION 3: NO CLAIM BONUS (NCB)

Rewarded for every consecutive claim-free year:

| Claim-Free Years | NCB Discount on OD Premium |
|---|---|
| 1 year | 20% |
| 2 consecutive years | 25% |
| 3 consecutive years | 35% |
| 4 consecutive years | 45% |
| 5+ consecutive years | 50% (maximum) |

**NCB Portability**: NCB belongs to the owner, not the vehicle. On selling the vehicle, NCB can be transferred to a new vehicle with an NCB Certificate.

**Grace Period**: If the policy lapses, NCB is protected for **90 days** from expiry date. Renewal after 90 days resets NCB to zero.

## SECTION 5: STANDARD ADD-ONS AVAILABLE WITH COMPREHENSIVE PLAN

### Add-On 1: Zero Depreciation (Nil-Dep / Bumper-to-Bumper Cover)
- No depreciation deducted on any replaced parts.
- Full part replacement at 100% cost — only compulsory deductible payable.
- Maximum **2 Zero Dep claims** per policy year.
- Available for vehicles up to **5 years old**.

### Add-On 2: Hydrostatic Engine & Gearbox Protection (Engine Protect)
- Covers damage to internal engine parts, gearbox, and differential caused by water ingression (hydrostatic lock) during floods.
- Also covers damage from lubricant oil leakage due to accidental impact.
- Standard policies exclude water ingression without this add-on.

### Add-On 3: 24x7 Roadside Assistance (RSA)
- Free towing up to **50 km** to nearest authorized workshop.
- On-site minor mechanical/electrical breakdown repair.
- Flat tyre puncture repair and spare wheel change.
- Battery jump-start.
- Emergency fuel delivery (up to 5 litres; fuel cost at actuals).
- Locked key / key lockout assistance.

### Add-On 4: Return to Invoice (RTI) Cover
- On CTL or complete theft: Insurer pays the **original ex-showroom invoice price** of the vehicle plus registration fees and road tax — not just the depreciated IDV.

### Add-On 5: Tyre Protect Cover
- Covers tyre and tube damage independent of accident (cut, burst, sidewall damage).
- Labour charges for tyre change covered.

### Add-On 6: Consumables Cover
- Covers the cost of consumable items used during repair — nuts, bolts, screws, grease, engine oil, coolant, brake fluid, air conditioner gas, etc. (normally excluded from standard claims).

### Add-On 7: Key and Lock Replacement
- Covers the cost of replacing lost, stolen, or damaged vehicle keys and locks.

## SECTION 6: WHAT IS NOT COVERED (EXCLUSIONS)

1. **Driving under influence**: Alcohol, drugs, or intoxicants at the time of accident.
2. **Unlicensed driver**: Vehicle driven by a person without a valid, effective driving licence.
3. **Mechanical/Electrical breakdown**: Wear and tear, normal breakdown not caused by an insured peril.
4. **Consequential loss**: Any loss that is indirect, including loss of use of the vehicle.
5. **Tyre damage (standalone)**: Tyre/tube damage unless the vehicle is simultaneously damaged in the same accident (unless Tyre Protect Add-On is purchased).
6. **Use outside permitted purpose**: Racing, speed testing, hire and reward use, or carrying passengers beyond registered seating capacity for commercial purposes.
7. **Engine damage due to water ingression**: Without Engine Protection Add-On, damage from driving through flooded roads (hydrostatic lock) is excluded.
8. **Outside India**: Loss or damage occurring outside geographical limits of India.
9. **Nuclear / War risks**: Any loss attributable to nuclear weapons, war, invasion, or civil war.
10. **Agreed accessories not listed**: Electrical or non-electrical accessories fitted to the vehicle but not declared at policy inception are not covered.

## SECTION 7: CLAIMS PROCEDURE

### 7.1 Intimation Timeline
- **Accidental damage claims**: Notify Company within **48 hours** via 24x7 helpline.
- **Theft claims**: File FIR immediately with Police; intimate Company within **24 hours** of discovering theft.

### 7.2 Surveyor Inspection
- IRDAI-certified surveyor appointed within **24 hours** of claim registration.
- Surveyor inspects vehicle, photographs damage, and approves repair estimate before garage commences work.
- For claims under ₹50,000 — AI/App-based self-survey may be permitted.

### 7.3 Cashless Repair at Network Garages
1. Drive or tow vehicle to any of the **6,800+ authorized cashless network garages**.
2. Garage initiates claim with insurer.
3. Surveyor-approved repair begins immediately.
4. Insured pays only: Compulsory deductible + Non-covered consumables (unless add-ons cover them).
5. Insurer directly settles the balance repair bill with the garage.

### 7.4 Reimbursement at Non-Network Garage
1. Get surveyor approval before/during repair.
2. Collect original repair invoice, spare parts bills, labour charges receipt.
3. Submit to Company within **30 days** of repair completion.
4. Reimbursement credited to bank account within **7–10 working days**.
