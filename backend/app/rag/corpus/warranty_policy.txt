WARRANTY AND SERVICE GUARANTEE POLICY

Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Document ID: POL-WARRANTY-001

1. EQUIPMENT WARRANTY
1.1 Router and Modem
All company-provided routers and modems carry a 1-year limited hardware warranty from the date of installation. This covers manufacturing defects and hardware failures under normal use.

1.2 What is Covered
- Hardware failure due to manufacturing defect.
- Component failure not caused by physical damage, liquid, or misuse.
- Software/firmware issues that cannot be resolved remotely.

1.3 What is NOT Covered
- Physical damage (drops, cracks, liquid damage).
- Damage due to power surges (use a surge protector).
- Modifications by unauthorised personnel.
- Normal wear and tear.
- Loss or theft.

2. WARRANTY CLAIM PROCESS
To raise a warranty claim:
- Contact our support team within the warranty period.
- An engineer will diagnose the issue remotely first (typically within 24 hours).
- If the issue cannot be resolved remotely, a field visit will be arranged within 48 hours.
- Defective equipment will be replaced free of charge within the warranty period.
- Out-of-warranty replacement cost: Rs.2,500 for standard routers; Rs.4,000 for advanced routers.

3. SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT (SLA)
3.1 Fault Resolution Targets
- Priority 1 (Total service loss): Resolution within 24 hours.
- Priority 2 (Severely degraded service): Resolution within 48 hours.
- Priority 3 (Minor degradation): Resolution within 5 business days.

3.2 Engineer Visit SLA
- Standard priority: Next business day between 9 AM - 6 PM.
- High priority: Same day if reported before 2 PM, else next morning.

3.3 Compensation for SLA Breach
If the SLA is breached due to our fault (not external factors such as weather or infrastructure damage):
- Pro-rated service credit for the affected period.
- Additional compensation of Rs.200 per day for delays beyond 48 hours.

4. PLANNED MAINTENANCE
- Planned maintenance is notified at least 72 hours in advance via SMS and email.
- Maintenance windows are scheduled between 2 AM - 6 AM to minimise disruption.
- Planned maintenance does not count toward SLA breach calculations.

5. FORCE MAJEURE
Warranty and SLA obligations are suspended during force majeure events including:
- Natural disasters, floods, earthquakes.
- Government-mandated network outages.
- Third-party infrastructure failures outside our control.
