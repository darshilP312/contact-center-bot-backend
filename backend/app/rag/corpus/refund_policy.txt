REFUND POLICY

Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Document ID: POL-REFUND-001

1. ELIGIBILITY FOR REFUND
A refund may be requested under the following circumstances:
- Duplicate or incorrect charge on your bill.
- Service unavailability: If the service was unavailable for more than 48 continuous hours due to a fault on our part, a pro-rated refund for the affected period will be issued.
- Overpayment: Any overpayment relative to the applicable plan price.
- Cancellation within cooling-off period (see Cancellation Policy POL-CANCEL-001).

2. REFUND AMOUNTS AND LIMITS
- Refunds of up to Rs.10,000 are processed automatically upon verification.
- Refunds exceeding Rs.10,000 require manager approval and will be processed within 5 business days of approval.
- Pro-rated refunds are calculated on a daily basis: Monthly charge divided by 30 days.

3. REFUND TIMELINE
- Credit/debit card: 3-5 business days after approval.
- UPI / Net banking: 2-3 business days after approval.
- Cheque: 7-10 business days after approval.

4. HOW TO REQUEST A REFUND
- AI Contact Centre: Say "I need a refund" and the system will guide you through the process.
- Phone: Call 1800-XXX-XXXX and speak to a billing specialist.
- Email: billing@company.com with subject "REFUND REQUEST - [Account Number] - [Invoice Number]"

5. WHAT IS NOT REFUNDABLE
- Usage-based charges already consumed (data packs, add-on services used).
- Late payment fees.
- Physical equipment (routers, modems) unless defective within warranty period.
- Processing fees for bounced cheques.

6. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
If your refund request is denied and you disagree with the decision:
- First: Request a review by a senior billing specialist.
- Second: File a formal complaint via the grievance portal within 30 days.
- Third: Escalate to the regulatory authority (TRAI) if unresolved within 30 days of complaint.

7. REFUND STATUS TRACKING
Track your refund status using the reference number provided at the time of request via:
- SMS: Send "REFUND [Reference Number]" to 56789
- Online portal: My Account > Billing > Refund Status
