CANCELLATION POLICY

Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Document ID: POL-CANCEL-001

1. CANCELLATION WITHIN 30 DAYS (COOLING-OFF PERIOD)
Customers may cancel their subscription within 30 days of the service activation date without any penalty. A full refund of any advance payment made will be processed within 5-7 business days. This applies to all residential and small business plans.

2. CANCELLATION AFTER 30 DAYS
After the initial 30-day period, cancellation is subject to the following terms:
- Monthly plans: Cancel anytime with 30 days written notice. No early termination fee applies.
- Annual plans: Early termination fee of Rs.1,500 applies if cancelled before the contract end date.
- 2-Year plans: Early termination fee of Rs.3,000 applies if cancelled in the first year; Rs.1,500 if cancelled in the second year.

3. HOW TO CANCEL
Customers may cancel via:
- Phone: Call 1800-XXX-XXXX (toll-free, available 24/7)
- Email: cancel@company.com with subject "CANCELLATION REQUEST - [Account Number]"
- Online portal: My Account > Manage Subscription > Cancel Service

4. WHAT HAPPENS AFTER CANCELLATION
- Service continues until the end of the current billing period.
- All data, emails, and hosted content will be deleted 30 days after cancellation.
- Hardware (routers, modems) must be returned within 14 days or a replacement fee of Rs.2,500 will be charged.

5. EXCEPTIONS
The following scenarios allow immediate cancellation without fee:
- Service unavailability exceeding 72 continuous hours in a 30-day period.
- Price increase by more than 15% without 60 days prior notice.
- Verified relocation to an area not covered by the network.

6. WINBACK OFFER
Customers who cancel may be eligible for a winback offer within 90 days of cancellation. Contact customer support to enquire.
