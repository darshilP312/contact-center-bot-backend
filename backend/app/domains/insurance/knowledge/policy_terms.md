# Insurance Policy Terms and Conditions Summary

## 1. Definitions

**Insured**: The policyholder named in the policy schedule.

**Insured Vehicle/Property**: The vehicle or property described in the policy schedule.

**IDV (Insured Declared Value)**: The maximum amount payable in case of total loss or theft of the insured vehicle. Calculated as the manufacturer's listed selling price minus depreciation.

**Deductible (Excess)**: The amount the policyholder must pay out-of-pocket before the insurance company pays a claim. Also called "voluntary excess."

**Compulsory Deductible**: A mandatory deductible set by IRDAI that cannot be waived:
- Private cars up to 1500cc: ₹1,000
- Private cars above 1500cc: ₹2,000
- Two-wheelers: ₹100–₹200

**NCB (No Claim Bonus)**: A discount on renewal premium for each claim-free policy year.

**Endorsement**: A written amendment to the policy that modifies the terms, conditions, or coverage.

**Third Party**: Any person (not the insured or insurer) involved in an incident.

## 2. Policy Coverage Inclusions

### Motor Insurance — Comprehensive Policy Inclusions

**Section I — Own Damage**
The company will indemnify the insured against:
- Loss or damage to the insured vehicle caused by:
  - Accidental external means
  - Fire, explosion, self-ignition, or lightning
  - Burglary, housebreaking, or theft
  - Riot and strike
  - Earthquake (fire and shock damage)
  - Flood, typhoon, hurricane, storm, tempest, inundation, cyclone, hailstorm, frost
  - Accidental damage while in transit by road, rail, inland waterway, lift, elevator, or air
  - Terrorist activity (subject to applicable endorsement)

**Section II — Third-Party Liability**
- Liability for death or bodily injury to any person
- Liability for damage to property belonging to a third party
- Legal costs and expenses incurred with the company's consent

**Personal Accident Cover**
- Death of owner-driver: 100% of capital sum insured (up to ₹15,00,000)
- Permanent total disability: 100% of capital sum insured
- Permanent partial disability: As per schedule of benefits

## 3. Policy Exclusions

### General Exclusions (All Policy Types)

The company shall not be liable for:

1. **Consequential Loss**: Any loss, damage, or liability arising from consequential or indirect consequences of an insured event.

2. **Contractual Liability**: Liability assumed by the insured under any contract or agreement that would not otherwise exist.

3. **War and Nuclear Risks**: Loss or damage caused by war, invasion, act of foreign enemy, civil war, mutiny, nuclear weapons, or radioactive contamination.

4. **Criminal Acts**: Any claim arising from the intentional act of the insured or with the complicity of the insured.

5. **Unlicensed Driving**: Any claim where the driver does not hold a valid driving license for the class of vehicle at the time of the accident.

6. **Drunk Driving**: Any claim where the driver was under the influence of alcohol or drugs at the time of the accident (blood alcohol content exceeding 0.03%).

7. **Overloading**: Any claim arising from the vehicle being driven with a load exceeding the manufacturer's rated capacity.

### Motor Insurance Specific Exclusions

8. **Mechanical/Electrical Breakdown**: Damage due to mechanical, electrical, or electronic breakdown, failure, or wear and tear.

9. **Depreciation**: Normal depreciation of the insured vehicle.

10. **Tyre and Tube Damage**: Damage to tyres and tubes unless the vehicle was also damaged in the same incident.

11. **Consequential Damages**: Engine damage due to oil leakage, unless directly caused by the insured accident.

## 4. Claim Procedure

### Immediate Steps After an Incident

1. **Do NOT** leave the scene of an accident without exchanging information
2. Note down: third party vehicle number, name, contact, insurance details
3. Take photographs of the damage from multiple angles
4. For theft: file an FIR with police immediately
5. Notify our company within **24 hours** of the incident via:
   - AI assistant (available 24/7)
   - Helpline: 1800-XXX-XXXX
   - Website: insure.example.com/claims

### Document Requirements by Claim Type

**Vehicle Accident Claim**:
- Duly completed claim form
- Copy of driving license (original for verification)
- Copy of RC (Registration Certificate)
- Copy of policy document
- Police report (for third-party claims or accidents with injuries)
- Repair estimate from authorised garage
- Original repair bills and payment receipts

**Theft Claim**:
- Original FIR from police
- Non-Traceable Report (NTR) from police
- Copy of RC transferred to insurance company
- All sets of keys and service booklet
- Loan foreclosure NOC (if vehicle was under loan)

**Health/Medical Claim**:
- Completed claim form
- Original hospital bills
- Discharge summary
- Attending physician's certificate
- Investigation reports (X-rays, lab reports)
- Prescription copies

## 6. Premium Payment Terms

### Payment Due Date
Premiums are due on or before the policy start date for new policies, and on or before the renewal date for existing policies.

### Grace Period
- **Annual Premium Policies**: 30-day grace period after due date
- **Monthly Premium Policies (ECS)**: 15-day grace period
- Coverage remains active during grace period
- Claims during grace period are payable only after receipt of premium

### Consequences of Non-Payment
- Policy lapses at end of grace period
- No claims will be payable for incidents during lapse period
- Reinstatement within 90 days: pay due premium + ₹500 reinstatement fee
- Reinstatement after 90 days: new application and fresh underwriting required

## 7. Cancellation Terms

### Free-Look Period (15 days)
For new policies, you may cancel within 15 days of receipt of policy documents for a full refund, subject to:
- Deduction of stamp duty (₹50–₹200 depending on state)
- Pro-rated premium for the period of cover already provided
- Proportionate premium for any additional riders cancelled

### Cancellation by Policyholder (After Free-Look Period)
- For motor and property: Refund of premium for remaining unexpired policy period minus short-rate table deduction (typically 25–35%)
- Minimum retention: 25% of annual premium or ₹1,000, whichever is higher

### Cancellation by Company
The company may cancel the policy by giving:
- **Motor/Property**: 7 days' notice (refund of pro-rata premium for unexpired period)
- **Health**: 30 days' notice (with full refund for any period where no claims were made)

## 8. Subrogation

After settling a claim, the insurance company is legally entitled to step into the insured's shoes and recover the claim amount from any third party who was responsible for the loss. The insured must cooperate in any such recovery proceedings.

---

## 9. Fraud and Misrepresentation

Any claim made fraudulently or in the event of misrepresentation, concealment, or false declaration by the insured, the policy shall be void from the date of such fraud, and all claims shall be forfeited. Legal action may be taken under applicable Indian laws.
