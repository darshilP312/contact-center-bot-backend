# Insurance FAQ — Knowledge Base for RAG

## Claims

### Q: How do I file an insurance claim?
To file a claim, you'll need your policy number, the date of the incident, and details about what happened. You can file a claim through our AI assistant, our website at insure.example.com/claims, or by calling our 24/7 helpline at 1800-XXX-XXXX. Our AI assistant can walk you through the process step by step and submit the claim on your behalf instantly.

### Q: What information do I need to file a claim?
You will need:
1. Your policy number (found on your policy document or ID card)
2. Date of the incident
3. Type of incident (accident, theft, damage, medical, etc.)
4. Estimated damage amount
5. Any relevant documents (police report for theft, medical bills for health claims, repair estimates for property/vehicle)

### Q: How long does it take to process a claim?
- **Simple claims** (minor damage, clear documentation): 3–7 business days
- **Survey-required claims** (major vehicle/property damage): 7–15 business days after surveyor assessment
- **Complex claims** (disputes, investigation required): Up to 30 business days
- **Medical reimbursement**: 10–15 business days after all documents are submitted

### Q: What is the claim settlement process?
1. Claim registered → Claim ID issued
2. Documents verified by our team
3. Surveyor assigned (for major claims)
4. Assessment completed
5. Claim approved/rejected with reason
6. Payment processed to your registered bank account
7. Confirmation sent via SMS and email

### Q: Can I track my claim status?
Yes! You can check your claim status by:
- Asking our AI assistant with your Claim ID
- Visiting insure.example.com/track-claim
- Calling our helpline with your Claim ID (format: CLM-XXXXXX)

### Q: What if my claim is rejected?
If your claim is rejected, you will receive a detailed explanation via email and SMS. You have the right to:
1. Request a review within 30 days of rejection
2. Submit additional documentation to support your claim
3. Escalate to our grievance redressal team
4. Approach the Insurance Ombudsman if unresolved

## Policy Coverage

### Q: What does my comprehensive car insurance cover?
Comprehensive car insurance covers:
- **Own Damage**: Damage to your car from accidents, fire, theft, flood, storm, riots
- **Third-Party Liability**: Damage or injury you cause to others
- **Personal Accident Cover**: For driver and co-passengers (up to policy limits)
- **Natural Calamities**: Floods, earthquakes, cyclones

Comprehensive insurance does NOT cover:
- Normal wear and tear
- Mechanical/electrical breakdown
- Depreciation on parts (unless Zero Depreciation add-on purchased)
- Drunk driving incidents

### Q: What is Zero Depreciation cover?
Zero Depreciation (also called Bumper-to-Bumper) cover ensures you receive the full cost of repairs without any deduction for depreciation on parts. Without this add-on, you typically receive 50% of rubber/plastic parts cost and 80% for metal parts. We recommend this for cars under 5 years old.

### Q: What is NCB (No Claim Bonus)?
No Claim Bonus (NCB) is a discount on your renewal premium for each claim-free year:
- 1 claim-free year: 20% discount
- 2 claim-free years: 25% discount
- 3 claim-free years: 35% discount
- 4 claim-free years: 45% discount
- 5 or more claim-free years: 50% discount

**Important**: Filing a claim in any year resets your NCB to 0%.

### Q: What does home insurance cover?
Home insurance typically covers:
- **Structure**: Damage to building from fire, earthquake, flood, storm
- **Contents**: Furniture, appliances, electronics (with Contents cover)
- **Liability**: Accidental injury to visitors on your property
- **Temporary Accommodation**: Hotel costs if your home becomes uninhabitable

Optional add-ons:
- Valuables cover (jewellery, art, collectibles)
- Domestic help liability
- Rent loss cover

### Q: Is there a waiting period for new policies?
- **Car Insurance**: No waiting period. Coverage starts from policy start date.
- **Health Insurance**: Pre-existing conditions have a 2-4 year waiting period depending on the condition.
- **Critical Illness**: 90-day initial waiting period for most conditions.
- **Home Insurance**: No waiting period.

## Premium & Billing

### Q: How is my premium calculated?
Car insurance premium depends on:
- **IDV (Insured Declared Value)**: Market value of your car (higher IDV = higher premium)
- **Car age and model**: Older cars have higher premiums
- **City of registration**: Metro cities have higher premiums
- **Previous claims history (NCB)**
- **Add-ons selected**

### Q: When is my premium due?
Your premium renewal date is stated on your policy document. You will receive reminders:
- 30 days before expiry via SMS and email
- 15 days before expiry
- 3 days before expiry
- On the day of expiry

### Q: What payment methods are accepted?
We accept:
- UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM)
- Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex)
- Debit cards
- Net banking (all major Indian banks)
- EMI (for premiums above ₹5,000)

### Q: Can I pay premium in instalments?
Yes! For annual premiums above ₹5,000, you can pay in:
- **Quarterly** (3 instalments): 2% additional charge
- **Monthly** (12 instalments, via ECS/NACH mandate): 5% additional charge

### Q: What happens if I miss a premium payment?
- **30-day grace period**: For annual policies. Your policy remains active with a 30-day grace period.
- **After grace period**: Policy lapses. Claims during lapse period are not covered.
- **Reinstatement**: Policies lapsed within 90 days can be reinstated by paying the due premium plus a reinstatement fee.

## Renewals

### Q: When should I renew my policy?
You should renew your policy before the expiry date. We recommend starting the renewal process at least 7 days before expiry to avoid any coverage gap. If your policy expires, you may need a fresh inspection before renewal.

### Q: Can I switch from third-party to comprehensive during renewal?
Yes, you can upgrade from third-party to comprehensive insurance during renewal. The premium will be adjusted, and the vehicle may need an inspection if it's more than 3 years old.

### Q: What documents are needed for renewal?
For most renewals, no documents are needed — just your policy number and payment. You may need an inspection report if:
- The vehicle is more than 3 years old and upgrading from third-party
- There has been a significant claim in the previous year

### Q: Will my premium change at renewal?
Premiums can change at renewal due to:
- Age of the vehicle (IDV reduces each year)
- Changes in coverage or add-ons
- NCB earned or lost (if you filed a claim)
- Industry-wide tariff changes by IRDAI

## Surveyor & Inspection

### Q: When is a surveyor assigned for my claim?
A surveyor is assigned for:
- Vehicle damage claims above ₹10,000
- Property damage claims above ₹25,000
- All major structural damage claims

For smaller claims, we may do a cashless settlement directly with the garage or hospital.

### Q: How long does the surveyor take to visit?
Surveyors are typically assigned within 24–48 hours of claim registration. The actual visit is scheduled based on your availability, usually within 3–5 business days.

## Cancellation & Refunds

### Q: Can I cancel my policy?
Yes, you can cancel your policy at any time. The refund amount depends on:
- **Cancelled within 15 days of purchase (free-look period)**: Full refund minus stamp duty and proportional premium
- **Cancelled after free-look period**: Pro-rated refund for unused period, minus cancellation charges (typically ₹500–₹1,000)

### Q: How long does a refund take?
Approved refunds are processed within 7–10 business days to your original payment method (card/UPI/bank account).
