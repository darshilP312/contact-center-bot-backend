## SECTION 2: WHAT IS COVERED

### 2.1 Building Structure Cover

**Building Sum Insured**: Up to **₹2,00,00,000 (₹2 Crore)**

Settlement Basis: **Full Reinstatement Value** — the cost of rebuilding/restoring the structure to its pre-loss condition using new materials and current construction rates, without any age or depreciation deduction.

**All perils from Basic plan are included**, plus:

1. **Accidental Damage to Building** — covers sudden, unexpected accidental physical damage (e.g., water pipe bursts causing wall collapse, accidental breakage of fixed glass panels, mirror, sanitary ware).
2. **Subsidence and Heave** — extended cover for land movement causing structural cracks or foundation damage.
3. **Malicious Damage by Tenants** — if the insured owns the property and has rented it out, damage caused by tenants is covered.
4. **Architects' and Surveyors' Fees** — professional fees for damage assessment, architect redrawing plans, and rebuild supervision included within building sum insured.
5. **Debris Removal Costs** — cost of clearing debris after fire, flood, or earthquake up to 5% of building sum insured.
6. **Loss of Rent** — If the insured home is rendered uninhabitable due to an insured peril:
   - **Own accommodation**: Covered up to **₹2,00,000** in total for alternative accommodation.
   - **Rental income loss for landlords**: Covered up to **₹50,000 per month for maximum 6 months**.

### 2.2 Household Contents Cover

**Contents Sum Insured**: Up to **₹25,00,000 (₹25 Lakhs)**

Settlement Basis: **New-for-Old Reinstatement** — full cost of purchasing a brand-new equivalent replacement without any depreciation deduction.

**All contents from Basic plan included**, plus:

**High-Value Jewellery and Valuables (Floater):**
- Gold jewellery, silver items, diamond jewellery, precious gemstones, pearls
- Watches (luxury watches — Rolex, Omega, Cartier class)
- Covered up to **₹10,00,000 (₹10 Lakhs)** per policy year
- Individual item limit: ₹2,00,000 per item without schedule; items above ₹2,00,000 must be individually declared and valued

**Fine Art, Antiques & Collectibles:**
- Oil paintings, watercolours, sculptures, photographs
- Rare coins, stamps, and collectibles
- Covered up to **₹5,00,000** total
- Items above ₹50,000 individually must be professionally appraised and declared

**Gadget Cover:**
- Mobile phones (up to 3 per household)
- Tablets and e-Readers
- Portable gaming consoles
- Covered up to **₹1,50,000** per item, **₹3,00,000** total per year
- Covers accidental damage and theft even outside the home (in India)

**Musical Instruments:**
- Covered up to **₹2,00,000** for instruments stored at home

**Sports Equipment:**
- Cricket equipment, bicycle (premium cycle), golf clubs, skiing equipment
- Covered up to **₹1,00,000**

### 2.3 Jewellery In-Transit Cover
Jewellery being carried from home to a bank locker, jeweller, or worn at social occasions is covered against theft or accidental loss up to **₹3,00,000 per occurrence** while in transit within India.

### 2.4 Sneak Theft (Without Forced Entry)
Unlike the Basic plan, **Home Protector Elite** covers theft even when no forced entry (break-in) is evident — including:
- Theft by domestic workers (maid, cook, driver) where FIR is filed.
- Opportunistic theft when a door/window was left open.
- Subject to FIR and Police investigation report being submitted.

### 2.5 Global Contents Cover (World-Wide for Portable Items)
Portable personal belongings (laptop, jewellery, gadgets, camera) are covered even when outside the insured premises — **anywhere in India or abroad**:
- **India**: Up to **₹5,00,000** per incident.
- **International**: Up to **₹2,00,000** per incident (emergency only).
- Covers theft and accidental damage.

### 2.6 Domestic Appliance Breakdown (No Accident Required)
Unlike standard policies, **Home Protector Elite** covers sudden, unexpected electrical or mechanical breakdown of domestic appliances without a separate insured peril (no fire or flood required):
- Refrigerator, Air Conditioner, Washing Machine
- Geyser, Microwave, Oven
- Television, Home Theatre
- Covered for **repair or replacement** up to **₹1,00,000 per year**
- Applicable for appliances up to 7 years old

### 2.7 Third-Party Public Liability (Personal Liability)
If a guest, visitor, or domestic worker suffers **bodily injury** or **property damage** within your home and holds you legally liable, the Company pays:
- Legal defence costs.
- Compensation as awarded by court.
- Covered up to **₹25,00,000 (₹25 Lakhs)** per occurrence.

Examples: Guest falls from stairs due to broken railing; domestic worker is scalded by hot water pipe burst; visitor's car damaged by falling tree from your compound.

### 2.8 Data Recovery Cover
If computer/laptop data is lost due to an insured peril (fire, flood, power surge, theft), professional data recovery services are covered up to **₹25,000 per policy year**.

### 2.9 Emergency Accommodation
If home is uninhabitable due to an insured peril:
- **₹3,000 per day**, maximum **₹60,000 total** — significantly higher than Basic plan.

### 2.10 Emergency Repair
Emergency repairs to prevent further loss: Covered up to **₹25,000 per claim**.

### 2.11 Temporary Storage
If belongings need to be stored in a warehouse/storage facility during home reconstruction after an insured loss, storage costs covered up to **₹20,000**.

## SECTION 3: WHAT IS NOT COVERED (EXCLUSIONS)

### 3.1 Building Exclusions
1. Land value — never insurable.
2. Gradual deterioration, wear and tear, or aging of the structure.
3. Construction or design defects present at the time of policy inception.
4. Properties left **vacant for more than 90 days** continuously.
5. War, nuclear/biological/chemical warfare.

### 3.2 Contents Exclusions
1. **Cash, currency, and negotiable instruments** (cheques, demand drafts, bonds).
2. **Motor vehicles** (car, two-wheeler, three-wheeler) — insured separately.
3. **Animals and pets** — living things are not insured.
4. **Business goods, stock-in-trade, commercial equipment**.
5. **Deliberately damaged items** — wilful destruction by insured or family member.
6. **Items already in damaged condition** at policy inception.
7. **Loss of data / software** beyond the Data Recovery limit (₹25,000).

### 3.3 Jewellery Specific Exclusions
1. Unexplained disappearance or mysterious loss of jewellery (cannot be explained as theft or accident).
2. Loss of loose gemstones (unmounted precious stones).
3. Deliberate leaving behind / abandonment of jewellery.
4. Jewellery not declared and valued above the ₹2,00,000 individual item limit.

### 3.4 Gadget Cover Exclusions
1. Damage from gradual software failure, OS failure, or virus.
2. Cosmetic damage (scratches, dents that do not affect functionality).
3. Loss due to negligent or intentional damage by the insured.
4. Damage while gadget is in the custody of a courier or carrier.

## SECTION 4: CLAIMS PROCEDURE

### 4.1 Intimation
- **All claims**: Notify SecureShield Elite Claims Desk within **24 hours** of discovery of loss.
- **Theft / Burglary**: File FIR within **12 hours** + notify SecureShield simultaneously.
- **Fire**: Contact Fire Brigade + notify SecureShield within **12 hours**.

### 4.2 Surveyor Dispatch
- **Priority Surveyor** dispatched within **24 hours** (vs. 48–72 hours in Basic plan).
- For contents only claims up to ₹2,00,000 — self-survey with photos may be accepted.
- For jewellery / art claims — Specialist valuer / assessor appointed.

### 4.3 Settlement
- Basis: **Reinstatement Value** (New-for-Old) for contents and building.
- Settlement target: Within **5–7 working days** of complete document submission.
- Direct bank transfer to registered account.

### 4.4 Dedicated Elite Claims Manager
Each Home Protector Elite policyholder is assigned a **dedicated claims relationship manager** who assists from loss intimation through final settlement.

### 4.5 Documents Required
- Completed claim form
- Police FIR (for theft, burglary, riot)
- Fire brigade / meteorological reports (for fire, flood, cyclone)
- Photographs and video of damage
- Original purchase bills, valuation certificates (for jewellery/art)
- Contractor repair/rebuild estimates
- Ownership or rental agreement proof
