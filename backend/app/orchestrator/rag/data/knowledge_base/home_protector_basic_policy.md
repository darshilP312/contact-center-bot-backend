## POLICY OVERVIEW & SPECIALTY

**Home Protector Basic** is an affordable entry-level home insurance policy designed to protect Indian households against sudden, unexpected physical damage to the building structure and its contents. Whether you own your flat, live in an independent house, or rent a property, this policy provides essential financial protection against fire, theft, natural disasters, and more.

**Key Specialty / USPs:**
- Covers both **Building Structure** and **Household Contents** (furniture, appliances, electronics)
- Protection against **fire, flood, theft, earthquake, and storm**
- **All-India coverage** — urban and rural properties
- Simple, paperless claim filing via app or helpline
- No requirement of home inspection for properties below ₹50 Lakhs value
- Coverage available for **owned and rented homes**

## SECTION 2: WHAT IS COVERED

### 2.1 Building Structure Cover

**Building Sum Insured**: Up to **₹30,00,000 (₹30 Lakhs)**

The physical structure of the insured home is covered against loss or damage caused by:

1. **Fire and Allied Perils**:
   - Fire (accidental, excluding bush fire within 50m from structure)
   - Lightning strike
   - Explosion or implosion
   - Aircraft damage (falling aircraft, aerial objects)
   - Impact damage by vehicles, animals, or falling trees

2. **Natural Disasters (Acts of God)**:
   - Flood, inundation, and storm surge
   - Cyclone, typhoon, hurricane, and tempest
   - Earthquake (including fire and shock damage from earthquake)
   - Landslide and rockslide
   - Hailstorm
   - Subsidence (ground sinking) and heave of land (limited coverage)

3. **Man-Made Perils**:
   - Riots and civil commotion
   - Strike damage (accidental damage during industrial action)
   - Malicious damage and vandalism
   - Impact from road vehicles (e.g., car ramming into boundary wall or gate)

4. **Water Damage**:
   - Bursting or overflowing of internal water pipes, tanks, or plumbing apparatus
   - Accidental discharge from sprinkler systems
   *(Note: Seepage through walls due to monsoon or construction defect is NOT covered)*

**What is Included in Building Structure:**
- Walls, roof, ceilings, floors
- Built-in fixed cupboards and wardrobes
- Permanent bathroom and kitchen fittings (tiles, shower, geyser fixed to wall, kitchen chimney)
- Doors, windows, glass panes (fixed)
- Boundary walls, gate, compound wall
- Staircases (for independent houses)
- Electrical wiring permanently fixed to the structure
- Plumbing and sewage lines within the property

### 2.2 Household Contents Cover

**Contents Sum Insured**: Up to **₹5,00,000 (₹5 Lakhs)**

Moveable household goods and personal belongings inside the home are covered against fire, theft, burglary, natural disaster, and other listed perils. Covered items include:

**Electronics & Appliances:**
- Television, Home Theatre System, Audio Equipment
- Refrigerator, Washing Machine, Dishwasher, Microwave Oven
- Air Conditioner (split and window units)
- Laptop and Desktop computers (domestic use)
- Home appliances (mixer-grinder, juicer, rice cooker, induction cooktop)

**Furniture & Furnishings:**
- Wooden and upholstered furniture (sofas, beds, dining table, chairs, wardrobes)
- Curtains, blinds, and window treatments
- Carpets and rugs
- Mattresses and bedding

**Personal Belongings:**
- Clothing and garments (up to ₹25,000 total)
- Books, documents, study material (up to ₹10,000)
- Kitchenware (pots, pans, crockery, cutlery)

**Covered Perils for Contents:**
- Fire, explosion, lightning
- Flood, cyclone, earthquake
- Riot, strike, malicious damage
- Theft following violent forced entry (burglary) — lock picking or break-in evidence required
- Water damage from burst pipes

### 2.3 Theft & Burglary Cover
- Covered for theft involving **forced entry** (broken lock, damaged door, window break-in).
- **Sneak theft** (where there is no evidence of forced entry or break-in) is **NOT covered** under Basic plan.
- Police FIR must be filed immediately for all theft claims.
- Items stolen from outside the home (car, office, travel) — **not covered**.

### 2.4 Temporary Accommodation Expenses
If the home becomes uninhabitable due to an insured peril (fire, flood, etc.), reasonable hotel / rental accommodation costs are covered up to:
- **₹1,000 per day**, maximum **₹15,000 total** per claim.

### 2.5 Emergency Repair
Emergency repair costs incurred immediately after an insured event to prevent further damage (e.g., emergency water pipe stoppage, boarding up broken windows after burglary) are covered up to **₹5,000 per claim**.

## SECTION 3: WHAT IS NOT COVERED (EXCLUSIONS)

### 3.1 Building Structure Exclusions
1. **Gradual deterioration**: Normal wear and tear, weathering, oxidation, and aging of the structure.
2. **Construction defects**: Damage arising from faulty design, defective materials, or poor workmanship.
3. **Seepage and dampness**: Water seeping through walls, damp patches, mould/fungal growth from chronic moisture.
4. **Rodent and pest damage**: Damage by rodents, termites, insects, or vermin.
5. **Electrical/mechanical breakdown**: Failure of equipment not caused by a listed insured peril.
6. **Under-construction**: Properties under construction or major renovation.
7. **Vacant property**: Property left unoccupied for more than **60 consecutive days** — cover suspended until occupied.
8. **Land value**: The land on which the property is built is never covered.

### 3.2 Contents Exclusions
1. **High-value jewellery and gold**: Jewellery, gold coins, silverware, precious stones — **NOT covered** under Basic plan.
2. **Cash and currency**: Money, currency notes, stamps, cheques, bank drafts — not covered.
3. **Works of art, antiques, and collectibles**: Paintings, sculptures, rare collectibles.
4. **Motor vehicles and two-wheelers**: Vehicles (insured separately under motor policy).
5. **Business goods and stock**: Any goods, merchandise, or equipment used for commercial/business purposes.
6. **Pets and livestock**: Animals are not covered.
7. **Documents and data**: Loss of computer data, software, or important personal documents.
8. **Sneak theft**: Theft without evidence of forced entry.
9. **Items outside the home**: Belongings lost outside the residence premises.
10. **Intentional damage**: Damage caused by the insured or family members deliberately.

### 3.3 General Exclusions
- War, nuclear, biological, or chemical attacks.
- Government requisition or confiscation of property.
- Pollution or gradual contamination.
- Loss due to failure to maintain the property.

## SECTION 4: CLAIMS PROCEDURE

### 4.1 Immediate Steps After a Loss
1. For **theft/burglary**: File Police FIR at the nearest Police Station within **24 hours**.
2. For **fire**: Contact local fire brigade + preserve evidence; do not alter the site before surveyor inspection.
3. Call SecureShield Claims Helpline: **1800-XXX-XXXX** within **48 hours** of the incident.
4. Do not carry out repair work (beyond emergency repairs) without insurer's approval.

### 4.2 Documents Required
- Duly completed claim form
- Copy of police FIR (for theft, burglary, riot)
- Fire brigade report (for fire claims)
- Photographs of damaged property
- Original purchase bills / invoices of damaged contents
- Ownership proof (Sale Deed, Society share certificate, rental agreement)
- Original repair estimates from licensed contractors

### 4.3 Surveyor Inspection
- A surveyor is appointed within **48–72 hours** of claim registration.
- For claims above ₹1,00,000 — Insurer's designated expert assessor visits the property.
- For claims up to ₹1,00,000 — Self-assessment with photo documentation accepted.

### 4.4 Settlement
- Claim settled by cheque / bank transfer within **10–15 working days** after document verification.
- Basis of settlement: **Reinstatement Value** for building, **Actual Cash Value (ACV)** for contents (i.e., market value considering age and depreciation).
