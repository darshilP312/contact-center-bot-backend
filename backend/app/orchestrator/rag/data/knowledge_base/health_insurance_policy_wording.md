# SECURESHIELD HEALTH INSURANCE COMPANY LIMITED
## HEALTH GOLD COMPREHENSIVE INDEMNITY POLICY — POLICY TERMS & CONDITIONS
**Policy Form IRDAI/HLT-IND/H-GOLD/V.4/2024-25**

---

### SECTION 1: INPATIENT HOSPITALIZATION COVERAGE

#### Clause 1.1: Inpatient Medical Expenses
If an Insured Person contracts any disease or suffers accidental bodily injury during the Policy Year requiring continuous inpatient hospitalization for a minimum of 24 consecutive hours, the Company will indemnify the reasonable and customary charges incurred for:
1. **Room Rent, Boarding & Nursing Charges**: Covered up to **Single Private Air-Conditioned (AC) Room** category without any daily sub-limit or capping.
2. **Intensive Care Unit (ICU) / ICCU Charges**: Covered at **100% of actual charges** with **Zero Sub-limits and No Capping**.
3. **Medical Practitioner & Specialists**: Consultation, surgeon, anaesthetist, medical specialist, and intensivist fees.
4. **Operation Theatre & Diagnostic Charges**: Surgical appliances, OT charges, anaesthesia, blood, oxygen, diagnostic tests, lab investigations, pathology, X-ray, MRI, CT scans, and dialysis.
5. **Medicines, Drugs & Consumables**: Prescribed pharmaceuticals and drugs administered during inpatient stay.

#### Clause 1.2: Upgraded Room Category Proportionate Deduction Rule
If the Insured voluntarily opts for a room category higher than Single Private AC Room (e.g. Deluxe Room, Super Deluxe, or Suite), the Insured shall bear the proportionate difference in room rent and associated medical expenses resulting from such higher room category tariffs.

---

### SECTION 2: PRE-HOSPITALIZATION AND POST-HOSPITALIZATION EXPENSES

#### Clause 2.1: Pre-Hospitalization Medical Expenses
The Company will indemnify medical expenses incurred up to **60 days immediately preceding the date of hospital admission**, provided that:
1. Such medical expenses are incurred for the same illness, condition, or injury for which inpatient hospitalization occurred.
2. The inpatient hospitalization claim has been admitted and approved under Section 1.

#### Clause 2.2: Post-Hospitalization Medical Expenses
The Company will indemnify medical expenses incurred up to **90 days immediately following the date of discharge from the hospital**, provided that:
1. Such expenses relate to follow-up consultations, diagnostic monitoring, and prescribed medications for the condition treated during hospitalization.
2. Original diagnostic reports, doctor prescription notes, and itemized pharmacy bills are submitted within 30 days of completion of post-hospitalization treatment.

---

### SECTION 3: DAY CARE PROCEDURES & SURGICAL ADVANCEMENTS

#### Clause 3.1: Day Care Treatment Scope
The minimum 24-hour hospitalization requirement is waived for over **540+ listed Day Care procedures and surgeries** that are completed within less than 24 hours in a hospital or day-care surgery centre due to modern technological advancements.

Key covered day care surgeries include:
- Eye surgery: Cataract extraction, Phacoemulsification, Vitrectomy, Glaucoma surgery.
- Oncology: Chemotherapy infusions, Targeted therapy, Radiotherapy sessions.
- Nephrology / Urology: Haemodialysis, Lithotripsy (ESWL) for kidney stones, Cystoscopy.
- Orthopaedics & ENT: Arthroscopic knee repair, Tonsillectomy, Tympanoplasty, Septoplasty.
- Gastrointestinal: Endoscopic polypectomy, Laparoscopic cholecystectomy, Appendectomy, Haemorrhoidectomy.

---

### SECTION 4: ALTERNATIVE SYSTEMS OF MEDICINE (AYUSH TREATMENT)

#### Clause 4.1: AYUSH Inpatient Indemnity
The Company covers inpatient medical hospitalization expenses for treatments undergone under alternative Indian medical systems:
- **A**yurveda, **Y**oga & Naturopathy, **U**nani, **S**iddha, and **H**omeopathy.

**Eligibility Conditions**:
1. The treatment must be undergone as an inpatient in a Government Hospital or an AYUSH Hospital accredited by Quality Council of India (QCI) / National Accreditation Board for Hospitals (NABH).
2. Pure wellness, spa, rejuvenation, massage therapy, or non-medical treatments are strictly excluded.

---

### SECTION 5: MATERNITY BENEFIT AND NEWBORN BABY COVER

#### Clause 5.1: Maternity Expenses
Maternity coverage becomes active after a continuous waiting period of **24 months (2 Policy Years)** under Health Gold.
1. Covers normal delivery expenses up to **INR 75,000** per delivery.
2. Covers Caesarean Section (C-Section) delivery expenses up to **INR 1,00,000** per delivery.
3. Maximum benefit payable for up to **two living children** or two deliveries across the policy lifetime.
4. Pre-natal and post-natal pregnancy-related consultations, scans, and tests are covered within the maternity limit.

#### Clause 5.2: Newborn Baby Cover
The newborn baby is covered as an insured dependant from **Day 1 of birth up to 90 days** within the mother's sum insured, including emergency neonatal care, congenital condition treatment, and mandatory birth vaccinations.

---

### SECTION 6: AMBULANCE CHARGES & RESTORATION BENEFIT

#### Clause 6.1: Road Ambulance Expenses
Emergency road ambulance service charges incurred for transporting the patient to the nearest hospital with adequate medical facilities are covered up to **INR 5,000 per hospitalization event**.

#### Clause 6.2: 100% Automatic Sum Insured Restoration
If the base Sum Insured and Cumulative Bonus are exhausted due to admitted claims during the Policy Year, the Company provides a **100% automatic reload/restoration** of the base Sum Insured once in a Policy Year for unrelated illnesses.

---

### SECTION 7: WAITING PERIODS AND PRE-EXISTING DISEASE (PED) CLAUSES

The Company is not liable to make payment for claims arising during the following waiting periods:

#### Clause 7.1: Initial 30-Day Waiting Period
Expenses related to the treatment of any illness contracted within the first **30 days** from the policy inception date. *(This waiting period does not apply to accidental injuries requiring emergency hospitalization).*

#### Clause 7.2: Pre-Existing Disease (PED) Waiting Period
Pre-existing conditions (such as Diabetes Mellitus, Hypertension, Chronic Kidney Disease, Thyroid disorders, Asthma, and Heart diseases diagnosed prior to policy inception) shall be covered only after **24 months (2 consecutive years)** of continuous coverage under Health Gold.

#### Clause 7.3: Specific Ailment 24-Month Waiting Period
The following listed treatments and surgeries have a mandatory **24-month waiting period** regardless of whether pre-existing or newly contracted:
- Cataract and eye ailments, Hernia, Hydrocele, Piles/Fistula/Fissure, Benign Prostatic Hypertrophy (BPH), Joint replacement surgery (unless due to accident), Gallstones and Kidney stones, Hysterectomy for non-malignant conditions, and Varicose veins.

---

### SECTION 8: PERMANENT EXCLUSIONS

The Company shall not indemnify expenses incurred for:
1. Cosmetic, aesthetic, or plastic surgery (unless reconstructive surgery necessitated by accidental injury or burns).
2. Dental treatment or surgery of any kind, unless requiring 24-hour hospitalization due to accidental jaw fracture.
3. Routine eye check-ups, spectacles, contact lenses, laser vision correction (LASIK for refractive error < 7.5 dioptres), hearing aids, and crutches.
4. Self-inflicted injuries, attempted suicide, or hazardous / adventure sports activities.
5. Substance abuse, drug addiction, alcohol de-addiction treatments, and psychiatric/rehabilitation care.
6. Infertility treatments, surrogate pregnancy, IVF, IUI, and assisted reproductive technologies.
7. Treatment taken outside the territorial boundaries of the Republic of India.

---

### SECTION 9: CASHLESS CLAIMS & REIMBURSEMENT PROCEDURE

#### Clause 9.1: Cashless Facility at Network Hospitals
1. **Planned Hospitalization**: Submit the Pre-Authorization request through the hospital's TPA Insurance Desk at least **48 hours prior to scheduled admission**. The Insurer processes cashless approval within **2 to 4 hours**.
2. **Emergency Hospitalization**: Inform the Insurer / TPA within **24 hours of emergency admission**.
3. **Network**: 100% cashless hospitalization across **7,500+ accredited network hospitals** across India.

#### Clause 9.2: Non-Network Reimbursement Settlement
If treated at a non-network facility, settle the hospital invoice in full, collect original Discharge Summary, itemized hospital bill, receipt, prescription, and diagnostic reports. Submit to the Company within **30 days from date of discharge**. Claim reimbursement is settled via direct bank transfer within **7 to 10 working days**.
