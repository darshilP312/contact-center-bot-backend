## POLICY OVERVIEW & SPECIALTY

**Health Shield Gold** is a comprehensive mid-tier health indemnity plan engineered for growing families, working professionals, and individuals seeking meaningful hospital coverage without coverage gaps. It eliminates room rent sub-limits, reduces waiting periods, and includes maternity coverage—a significant step up from Basic plans.

**Key Specialty / USPs:**
- **No room rent sub-limit** — Private Single AC Room covered in full
- **Zero ICU cap** — 100% ICU charges reimbursed
- **Maternity benefit** included (after 24-month waiting period)
- **Newborn baby cover from Day 1** of birth
- **540+ Day Care procedures** covered
- **100% Sum Insured Restoration** (once per year, for different illness)
- **7,500+ cashless network hospitals**
- Annual health check-up worth ₹2,500 included

## SECTION 2: WHAT IS COVERED

### 2.1 Inpatient Hospitalization (Minimum 24-Hour Stay)
1. **Room Rent, Boarding & Nursing**: Covered up to **Single Private Air-Conditioned (AC) Room** — no daily sub-limit or capping.
2. **ICU / ICCU Charges**: Covered at **100% of actual charges** — Zero sub-limits, No capping.
3. **Medical Practitioners & Specialists**: Consultation, surgeon, anaesthetist, medical specialist, and intensivist fees — fully covered.
4. **Operation Theatre & Diagnostic Charges**: OT charges, anaesthesia, blood, oxygen, MRI, CT scan, PET scan, pathology, lab investigations — fully covered.
5. **Medicines, Drugs & Consumables**: All prescribed pharmaceuticals and drugs during the inpatient stay.

### 2.2 Pre-Hospitalization Expenses
Medical expenses incurred up to **60 days before** the date of hospital admission for the same illness/injury are covered in full.

### 2.3 Post-Hospitalization Expenses
Follow-up consultations, prescribed medicines, and diagnostic monitoring incurred up to **90 days after discharge** are covered in full.

### 2.4 Day Care Procedures (540+ Listed Procedures)
Modern procedures completed within less than 24 hours are fully covered:
- **Eyes**: Cataract extraction (Phacoemulsification), Vitrectomy, Glaucoma surgery, Retinal photocoagulation
- **Oncology**: Chemotherapy infusions, Targeted therapy, Radiotherapy, Immunotherapy
- **Nephrology / Urology**: Haemodialysis, Lithotripsy (ESWL) for kidney stones, Cystoscopy, TURP
- **Orthopaedics & Spine**: Arthroscopic knee/shoulder repair, Epidural steroid injection
- **ENT**: Tonsillectomy, Adenoidectomy, Tympanoplasty, Septoplasty, Nasal polypectomy
- **Gastrointestinal**: Endoscopic polypectomy, Laparoscopic cholecystectomy, Haemorrhoidectomy, Colonoscopy with biopsy
- **Cardiology**: Cardiac catheterization (diagnostic), Cardioversion (elective)
- **Gynaecology**: D&C, Hysteroscopy, Colposcopy, Laparoscopic ovarian cystectomy
- **Dermatology**: Skin tag / mole excision requiring operating room, Laser treatment for Haemangioma

### 2.5 Maternity Benefit (After 24-Month Waiting Period)
1. **Normal Delivery**: Covered up to **₹75,000** per delivery.
2. **Caesarean Section (C-Section)**: Covered up to **₹1,00,000** per delivery.
3. **Pre-Natal & Post-Natal consultations**, prescribed scans, and tests covered within maternity limit.
4. Applicable for up to **two deliveries** across policy lifetime.

### 2.6 Newborn Baby Cover
The newborn is covered from **Day 1 of birth up to 90 days** within the mother's Sum Insured for:
- Emergency neonatal ICU care
- Congenital condition treatment
- Phototherapy for neonatal jaundice
- Mandatory birth vaccinations

### 2.7 AYUSH Treatment
Inpatient treatment at Government/NABH-accredited AYUSH hospitals (Ayurveda, Yoga, Unani, Siddha, Homeopathy) is covered within Sum Insured.

### 2.8 Ambulance Charges
Emergency road ambulance: Covered up to **₹5,000 per hospitalization event**.

### 2.9 100% Automatic Sum Insured Restoration
If the Sum Insured is exhausted during the policy year, it is automatically restored once per year (100%) for hospitalization due to a **different illness/injury** than the one that caused the original claim.

### 2.10 Annual Health Check-Up
Preventive annual health check-up (full blood count, lipid profile, blood sugar, urine analysis, ECG) covered up to **₹2,500 per year** at empanelled diagnostic labs — no hospitalization required.

### 2.11 Covered Diseases & Conditions
All diseases requiring inpatient care are covered (after applicable waiting periods):

**Cardiovascular:**
Heart Attack (Acute Myocardial Infarction), Unstable Angina, Coronary Artery Bypass Graft (CABG), Heart failure, Hypertensive crisis, Cardiac arrhythmias, Valvular heart disease, Peripheral artery disease

**Cancer / Oncology:**
All types of malignant cancers — Breast cancer, Lung cancer, Colon/Colorectal cancer, Cervical cancer, Blood cancers (Leukaemia, Lymphoma, Myeloma), Prostate cancer, Brain tumours

**Neurological:**
Stroke (Ischaemic & Haemorrhagic), TIA, Meningitis, Encephalitis, Parkinson's disease hospitalization, Multiple sclerosis flares, Epilepsy with status epilepticus, Guillain-Barré Syndrome

**Respiratory:**
Pneumonia, Severe Asthma (acute attack), COPD exacerbation, Pulmonary Embolism, Pulmonary Tuberculosis (TB), Pleural effusion, Lung abscess, COVID-19 pneumonia

**Renal / Urological:**
Acute Kidney Injury (AKI), Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) with dialysis, Nephrotic Syndrome, Pyelonephritis, Obstructive uropathy, Renal calculi requiring surgery

**Gastrointestinal:**
Acute Pancreatitis, Liver cirrhosis complications (Ascites, Variceal bleeding), Inflammatory Bowel Disease (Crohn's, Ulcerative Colitis), Peptic Ulcer disease, Intestinal obstruction, Cholecystitis, Appendicitis

**Musculoskeletal / Orthopaedic:**
Fractures (any bone), Hip/Knee replacement (post-accident, within 24-month wait for elective), Spine surgery, Osteomyelitis, Septic arthritis

**Endocrine / Metabolic:**
Diabetic Ketoacidosis (DKA), Hyperosmolar Hyperglycaemic State (HHS), Severe Hypoglycaemia requiring ICU, Thyroid storm (Thyrotoxicosis), Addison's crisis, Pituitary disorders

**Gynaecological & Obstetric:**
Ectopic pregnancy (emergency), Ovarian cysts (torsion/rupture), Uterine fibroids (surgical), Pre-eclampsia, Eclampsia, Postpartum haemorrhage, PCOS with complications

**Infectious & Vector-Borne:**
Dengue Haemorrhagic Fever, Malaria (complicated P. falciparum), Typhoid with complications, Scrub Typhus, Leptospirosis, Chikungunya with severe joint involvement, Rabies, Viral encephalitis

**Paediatric:**
Febrile convulsions, RSV bronchiolitis, Neonatal sepsis, Meningococcal disease, Intussusception, Thalassaemia (transfusion-dependent), Kawasaki disease

## SECTION 3: WHAT IS NOT COVERED (EXCLUSIONS)

### 3.1 Pre-Existing Disease (PED) Waiting Period
Pre-existing conditions (Diabetes, Hypertension, Heart disease, Thyroid, Asthma, CKD, and any condition diagnosed before policy start) are **not covered for the first 24 months** of continuous coverage.

### 3.2 Specific Ailment Waiting Period (24 Months)
- Cataract surgery & eye ailments
- Hernia (all types), Hydrocele
- Piles / Fistula / Fissure / Haemorrhoids
- Benign Prostatic Hypertrophy (BPH) / Prostate surgery
- Elective joint replacement (non-accidental)
- Gallstone / Kidney stone removal (unless emergency)
- Hysterectomy for non-malignant conditions
- Varicose veins / Varicose ulcers
- Tonsillectomy / Adenoidectomy (non-emergency)

### 3.3 Initial 30-Day Waiting Period
No illness-based claim (except accidental injury) within the first **30 days** from policy inception date.

### 3.4 Permanent Exclusions
1. Cosmetic or aesthetic surgery (unless necessitated by accident or burns).
2. Dental treatment (unless 24-hour hospitalization due to accidental jaw fracture).
3. Spectacles, contact lenses, hearing aids, LASIK for refractive error below 7.5 dioptres.
4. Self-inflicted injuries, suicide attempts.
5. Adventure / extreme sports injuries (skydiving, motor racing, mountaineering over 5,000m).
6. Substance abuse, alcoholism, drug addiction, and associated conditions.
7. Infertility, IVF, ICSI, IUI, surrogacy, and all assisted reproduction.
8. Treatment outside India (unless OPD emergency abroad rider is added).
9. OPD expenses (unless specifically covered add-on is purchased).
10. Experimental, unproven, or investigational treatments.
11. War, nuclear fallout, civil war, invasion, or terrorism (biological/chemical attacks).

## SECTION 4: CLAIMS PROCEDURE

### 4.1 Cashless Facility
- **Planned Hospitalization**: Pre-Authorization at least **48 hours before admission**. Insurer processes approval within **2–4 hours**.
- **Emergency**: Inform Insurer/TPA within **24 hours** of emergency admission.
- **Network**: 7,500+ accredited cashless network hospitals across India.

### 4.2 Reimbursement (Non-Network)
Collect Discharge Summary, itemized bills, prescriptions, and diagnostic reports. Submit within **30 days of discharge**. Settled by direct bank transfer within **7–10 working days**.
